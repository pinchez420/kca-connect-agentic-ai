## 1. INTRODUCTION, VISION, AND MISSION

### 1.1. General Information 
This Guide has been prepared for general information to all students. It does not constitute a contract and is subject to change at the University’s discretion. Students are advised to check the University Website, Official Facebook page (@KCAUniversityOfficial), and Notice Boards (located in Faculties, Dean of students’ office, and Tuition complex blocks) for updates.

### 1.2. Our Vision
To be recognized as a premier business university whose graduates competently and confidently serve the nation of Kenya, the East African region and the global community.

### 1.3. Our Mission
To offer high quality, integrated and market driven teaching, research and extension services in a safe and supportive academic environment.

### 1.4. Our Philosophy
It is the philosophy of the University that all human beings have a role to play in the society and that each person is uniquely endowed with the necessary abilities to fulfil their role.

### 1.5. Core Values
* Excellence - do it right first time.
* Integrity - carry out our business with honesty and openness.
* Diversity - no discrimination on the basis of ethnicity, gender, social, economic or religious backgrounds.
* Innovation - seek new ways of creating, sharing and applying knowledge.
* Commitment - committed to meeting the needs of our students, faculty, alumni and partners.
* Service to the Community - development and implementation of solutions to community problems.
* Recognition and support of individual talents.

### 1.6. Historical Background
KCA University dates back to 1989, established as Kenya College of Accountancy by ICPAK. Enrollment has grown from 170 students in 1989 to over 5,000 annually today. The main campus is on Thika Road at Ruaraka, with satellite campuses in Kitengela, Town Campus (Nairobi), Amagoro, and Kisumu.

## 3. CAMPUS LIFE AND STUDENT WELFARE

### 3.1. Student Association of KCA University (SAKU)
SAKU is the student governing body. It is governed by the SAKU Council, which is elected annually. Objectives include promoting student welfare, safeguarding rights, and nurturing leadership qualities.

### 3.2. Healthcare and Counseling
* **Counseling Center:** Located in Block K. Provides individual/group counseling and crisis intervention free of charge.
* **Health Services:** Clinic located in Block K for minor ailments. Complicated cases are referred to Avenue Health Care (at guardian's cost). Students are insured for injuries sustained during university sporting activities.

### 3.3. Facilities and Services
* **Cafeteria:** Situated next to the tuition complex. Operates on a pay-as-you-eat basis.
* **Accommodation:** The University does not provide on-campus housing but maintains a list of approved private hostel providers.
* **Transport:** University buses are available for authorized trips. Students must purchase vouchers from the Administration Managers office in Block B.

### 3.4. Clubs and Sports
* **Clubs:** Includes Wildlife, Journalism, Tech, Drama, Music, and St John’s Ambulance. Minimum 15 members required for registration.
* **Sports:** Football, Basketball, Rugby, Hockey, Volleyball, Karate, Tae Kwondo, Badminton, Table Tennis, and Swimming. Registration is handled by the Sports Coordinator.

## 4. ACADEMIC CONDUCT AND REGULATIONS

### 4.1. Academic Calendar
The Academic Year begins in September and ends in August. It consists of three trimesters or two semesters (15 weeks each).

### 4.2. Registration and Admissions
* **New Students:** Must present Admission letters and fill out registration forms. Names used must match National ID/Official documents.
* **Continuing Students:** Must register online via the Student Portal. Add/Drop period is limited to the first 2 weeks of the trimester.
* **Academic Leave:** Maximum period of two academic years. Application must be made by week six of the trimester.

### 4.3. Examinations
* **Class Attendance:** Compulsory. Students missing more than four weeks of a trimester may be disqualified.
* **Pass Requirements:** Must be enrolled, attend classes, and complete all assessments/exams to the satisfaction of the Senate.
* **Conduct:** No smart gadgets allowed. Calculators must be non-programmable. Lateness of more than 30 minutes is not allowed.
* **Special Exams:** Granted for bereavement (nuclear family) or medical reasons (with doctor's proof). Misreading the timetable is not a valid reason.
* **Appeals:** Remarking requests must be made within two weeks of result release and require a non-refundable fee.

## 6. STUDENT FINANCE AND FEES

### 6.1. Payment Requirements
Students must pay at least 50% of total fees at the start of the trimester to attend classes. Outstanding debts from previous trimesters prevent registration.

### 6.3. Financial Aid and Scholarships
1. **Work Study Program:** For full-time students with financial need. Max 20 hours per week.
2. **Chancellor’s Scholarship:** For students with KCSE grade A or A-.
3. **Founders Scholarship:** Merit-based for needy students.
4. **SAKU Bursary:** Maximum of Kshs. 5,000 for needy students.

### 6.4. Fee Refunds
Refunds are only made under special circumstances (death, medical incapacitation, or university-led discontinuation). A 10% administration fee is charged on all refundable amounts.
