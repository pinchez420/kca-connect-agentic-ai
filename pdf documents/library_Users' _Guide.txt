## 👋 Welcome

> Welcome to KCAU Library! This is a modern, user-focused study and learning resource center created and maintained to provide scholars with sufficient access to information resources that drives the teaching, research, and outreach mission of KCA University.

**Purpose of This Guide:**
- 🔍 Understand the role of KCA University Library in your learning activities
- 📚 Learn about information resources and services provided
- 📋 Familiarize yourself with registration, borrowing procedures, regulations, and opening hours
- 🛠️ Master basic techniques of retrieving information in the library

---

## 🎯 Library Mandate & Values

### Our Mandate
> To collect, manage, and curate world-class information resources with respect to the mandate, vision, and mission of KCA University.

### Our Philosophy
> Access to quality information resources is fundamental to academic excellence and a requisite support to lifelong learning.

### Our Vision
> To be a world-class modern academic research library enabling KCA University to become the Business University of choice.

### Our Mission
> To provide world-class and innovative service and learning space that will empower KCA University to carry out its core activities of teaching, learning, research, and outreach.

### Our Objectives

| Objective | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| 📚 Resource Acquisition | Select, acquire, and organize quality electronic and print information resources supporting learning, teaching, and research |
| ♿ Ethical Access | Promote access and ethical use of information resources |
| 💻 ICT Integration | Encourage use of modern ICT to enhance access to and delivery of information and services |
| 🤝 Strategic Partnerships | Engage with local, national, and international alliances to enhance wider access to information |
| 🪑 Physical Environment | Provide users with adequate physical space and maintain conducive reading conditions |

### Our Core Values

| Value | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 🤝 Customer Service | We value our users (customers) |
| 🙏 Courtesy | Respect and treat every user with courtesy |
| 🌍 Diversity | Embrace diversity in all its forms |
| ⚖️ Legitimacy | Observe intellectual property rights, copyright laws, and conventions |
| 🔓 Access | Provide fair access to resources |

### Core Functions

1. 📦 Select and procure comprehensive range of documents (books, journals, magazines, newspapers) supporting academic endeavors
2. 🗂️ Accession, classify, and catalog procured materials for ease of management and retrieval
3. 🌐 Establish and provide access to world-class electronic resources supporting in-depth research and learning
4. 🧘 Maintain a safe and conducive physical environment for study
5. 🎓 Provide information literacy training programs to help users maximize library services
6. 💬 Provide information and reference services to library users

## 🛎️ User Services

### Service Ethos
During interactions with library staff, users can expect to:

- ✅ Be acknowledged appropriately
- ✅ Be treated courteously and respectfully
- ✅ Be valued for their input
- ✅ Receive the same high standard of service regardless of age, race, ethnicity, religion, gender, physical limitations, or any other criteria
- ✅ Receive prompt and timely service
- ✅ Receive knowledgeable service and professionalism from all staff
- ✅ Have open access to traditional and innovative resources and instruction in their use
- ✅ Have their privacy and confidentiality respected

### Library Membership

| Eligible Members | Requirements |
|-----------------|--------------|
| 👨‍🎓 All current students | Valid Student ID |
| 👨‍🏫 All members of staff | Valid Staff ID |
| 🏛️ Governing Council members | University Board of Trustees affiliation |
| 📊 ICPAK Council members | Must be introduced by ICPAK librarian |

> ⚠️ The Librarian has the authority to withdraw membership of any individual found flouting library rules and regulations.

### Reference Services

The library provides both **in-person** and **virtual** reference services through:

- 🗣️ In-person inquiries
- 💬 Ask-a-Librarian
- 🎧 Live support
- 📞 Telephone inquiries
- 📧 Email support

### Computer-Based Information Services

> KCAU Library is a **Hybrid Library** existing in both physical and virtual spaces.

| Service | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| 🌐 E-resources Access | Remote access to subscribed databases and e-journals |
| 📶 Wi-Fi & Internet | Campus-wide connectivity for research |
| 💻 E-Center Computers | Designated computers for digital resource access |
| 📥 Downloading & Sharing | Authorized downloading of materials for academic use |

### Current Awareness Services (CAS)

The library utilizes webpages and social media platforms to notify users of:

- 📚 New book acquisitions
- 📢 Meeting announcements
- 🎓 Training courses and workshops
- 🗓️ Events of interest to users

### Circulation Policy

**Main Tenet**: Ensure the fullest exploitation of the library's collections.

| Guideline | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| 🔒 Non-Loanable Items | Rare, costly, format-specific, or reference materials |
| 📰 Current Periodicals | Journals, newspapers, atlases, maps, reference materials are non-loanable |
| 🔄 Loan Status Variation | May change based on demand |
| 💰 Overdue Fines | Levied to reinforce equitable circulation; rates subject to review |
| 🎯 Holds/Reservations | Users may place holds on checked-out materials |
| ⚡ Recalls | Library reserves right to recall loaned items before due date |

### Users with Special Needs

> The Library endeavors to offer appropriate support services in tandem with KCA University policies on persons with disabilities and Commission for University Education guidelines.

### Collaborative Learning

- 🗣️ Special discussion areas available (subject to space availability)
- 🤝 Users may collaborate on shared research activities
- 🔇 Private study areas maintained for individual work

### Collaboration in Collection Development

The Library partners with organizations, consortiums, agencies, and stakeholders to:

- 🌐 Fulfill its mandate through resource sharing
- 📈 Enhance access to facilities and resources of collaborating institutions
- ✅ Ensure all partnerships benefit the library and align with its mission

### Distance Learning Support

| Guideline | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| 🌐 Online Resources | Distance learners expected to use e-journals, e-books, databases via internet |
| 📧 Access Method | Via student's KCAU email account |
| ⚖️ Legal Compliance | Learners must be aware of internet restrictions and copyright laws in their territory |
| 🏢 In-Person Access | Distance learners may borrow in person with valid Student ID under standard terms |

### Information Literacy

> Information literacy is an understanding and set of abilities enabling individuals to recognize when information is needed and have the ability to locate, evaluate, and use effectively the needed information.

**Approaches Used:**
- 🎓 Compatibility with KCA University programs and regulations
- 🛠️ Various training methods to grow users' information skills

## 📚 Collections & Resources

### Collection Levels
The library collection is strategically built to support academic programs and research needs:

| Section | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| 📦 General Stack (Main Long) | Standard loan collection |
| ⚡ Short Loan | High-demand items with shorter loan periods |
| 📖 Reference | Non-loanable reference materials |

### Electronic Information Resources

> Prime preference given to acquisition of electronic materials due to increasing user preference.

| Resource Type | Examples |
|--------------|----------|
| 🔗 Online Journals | Links to subscribed e-journal databases |
| 💿 Electronic Information | E-books, databases, digital content on discs |

### Books
- 📋 Acquisition policy guides addition of materials
- 📊 Few copies placed in short-term loan section for equitable access

### Core Journals
- 🤝 Subscribed via **Kenya Library & Information Services Consortium (KLISC)**

### Magazines and Newspapers
- 📰 Selected magazine titles and leading Kenyan print newspapers available daily
- 🗓️ Older print newspaper issues kept for one calendar year, then disposed
