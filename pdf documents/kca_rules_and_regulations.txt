## 🎓 Academic Conduct

### 1.1 Class Attendance

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| ⏰ Punctuality | Students may **not be more than 5 minutes late** for class |
| 📝 Attendance Sheets | Must be signed for **each lesson** |
| ✍️ Name Accuracy | Ensure your **full correct name** appears on attendance sheet |

### 1.2 Tests & Assignments

| Policy | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| 📅 Scheduling | Tests and assignments must be done **as timetabled** |
| 🚫 Exemptions | Any exemption from sitting tests/examinations must be sought from **Office of the Registrar** well in advance |

## 💰 Fee Payment & Registration

### 1.3 Fee Payment Rules

> ⚠️ **Fee payment rules must be strictly observed.**

| Violation | Consequence |
|-----------|-------------|
| 🚫 Found in class without paid fees | **EXPULSION** *(unless proof of authority from Finance Director provided)* |
| 📋 Failure to register as current student each semester/trimester | Cannot access university facilities and services |
| 🎓 Attending classes not paid for | **SUSPENSION** + required to pay fees **in full** for that whole section |

**Key Requirements:**
- ✅ Students **MUST register** as current students every new semester/trimester
- ✅ Registration required to access facilities and services
- ✅ Proof of fee payment or Finance Director authorization mandatory for class attendance

## 🪪 Identification & Security

### 1.4 University Identification Card

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| 🎒 Carry Requirement | ID card **must be carried at all times**, including copies of fee receipts |
| 🚪 Entry Requirement | **No student allowed into University without Student ID** |
| 🔄 Card Exchange | Any student found **IN THE ACT OF EXCHANGING STUDENT ID CARDS** will be **PENALIZED and SUSPENDED** |
| 🆘 Lost Cards | Must be reported **immediately**; replacement cost: **Kshs. 600** |

> 🔐 Your Student ID is your key to university access and services.

## 🚫 Prohibited Conduct

### 1.5 Smoking & Drinking

| Prohibition | Consequence |
|-------------|-------------|
| 🚭 Smoking in compound | Face disciplinary committee + **SUSPENSION** |
| 🌿 Smoking drugs (e.g., bhang) | **EXPULSION** |
| 🍺 Drunkenness on campus | **Immediate SUSPENSION** |

> ❌ **Smoking and drinking are completely banned at KCAU.**

### 1.6 Fights, Brawls, Forgery & Theft

> Such cases will be treated as **criminal offences** and referred to the police.

| Offence | Process | Potential Outcome |
|---------|---------|------------------|
| ⚔️ Fights/Brawls | Referred to police + disciplinary committee | **EXPULSION or SUSPENSION** |
| 📜 Forgery | Referred to police + disciplinary committee | **EXPULSION or SUSPENSION** |
| 🎒 Theft | Referred to police + disciplinary committee | **EXPULSION or SUSPENSION** |

### 1.7 Mobile Phones and Walkmans

| Device | Rule |
|--------|------|
| 📱 Mobile Phones | **Must NOT be put on while in class at all times** |
| 🎧 Walkmans | May **only** be used in fields **out of class hours** |

### 1.8 Arrests by Police

> Students are governed by the overall laws of the land that govern other Kenyans.

| Scenario | Protocol |
|----------|----------|
| 🚔 Arrest on/off campus | Student should **immediately inform the University** |
| ⚖️ Police duties | University **will not interfere** with police carrying out duties in accordance with the law |

## 🗣️ Meetings & Public Statements

### 1.9 Meetings, Processions and Demonstrations

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| 🚫 Unauthorized gatherings | **Serious offence** to organize/participate in meetings, demonstrations, or processions without University permission |
| 🎊 Unauthorized ceremonies | Students should **not undertake** unauthorized ceremonies |

### 1.9.1 University Property

> A student or group of students will be held **responsible** for any damage to University property resulting from misuse or willful destruction.

### 1.9.2 Loss or Damage of Students' Property

> ⚠️ **The University will NOT be held responsible** for losses or damage to students' property while on University premises.

**Student Responsibility:**
- 🔐 Every student is expected to **take care of his/her valuables**

### 1.9.3 Fire Fighting Appliances

| Prohibition | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| 🚒 Interference | **Serious offence** to interfere with, damage, or remove fire fighting appliances *(except for fire-fighting purposes)* |

### 1.9.4 Emergency Protocol

**In case of illness, urgent crisis, etc.:**

1. 🩺 Report to: **Nurse**, **Security Officer**, or **Dean of Students**
2. 🔄 If above unavailable: Report to **Registrar** and respective **Deans of Faculties**

### 1.9.5 Correspondence & Public Statements

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| 📢 Public statements | No student shall make public statements on behalf of students association/societies on University matters **without specific authority** from relevant authority |
| 🎤 Invitations of dignitaries | Invitations of Government ministers, politicians, foreign representatives to visit University in official capacity **must be channeled through University authorities** |
