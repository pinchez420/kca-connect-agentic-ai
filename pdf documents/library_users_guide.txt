# KCA UNIVERSITY LIBRARY: USERS' GUIDE

## 1. MISSION AND MANDATE
The KCAU Library is a modern, user-focused learning resource center designed to support the teaching, research, and outreach mission of KCA University.

### 1.1. Core Mandate
To collect, manage, and cure world-class information resources with respect to the mandate, vision, and mission of KCA University.

### 1.2. Vision and Mission
* **Vision:** To be a world-class modern academic research library enabling KCA University to become the Business University of choice.
* **Mission:** To provide world-class and innovative service and learning space that will empower KCA University to carry out its core activities of teaching, learning, research and outreach.

### 1.3. Core Values
* Customer Service: We value our users (customers).
* Courtesy: Respect and treat every user with courtesy.
* Diversity: Embrace diversity in all its forms.
* Legitimacy: Observe intellectual property rights, copyright laws, and conventions.
* Access: Provide fair access to resources.

## 3. USER SERVICES AND MEMBERSHIP

### 3.1. Membership Eligibility
Library membership is open to:
* All current students
* All members of staff
* Governing Council members of the University
* Board of Trustees of the University
* ICPAK Council members (with introduction by ICPAK librarian)

### 3.2. Reference and Computer Services
The library provides both in-person and virtual reference services including "Ask-a-librarian" and live support.
Computer-based services include:
* E-resources access
* Wi-fi and internet access
* Use of designated computers at the E-center
* Downloading and sharing of research materials

### 3.3. Distance Learning Support
Distance learners access resources via the internet. Access is normally provided via a student’s email account. Distance learners visiting the library in person may borrow materials using a valid student ID card.

### 4.2. Finding Books
Books are located using alphanumeric "Call Numbers" found on the spine of the book. These numbers denote the subject division, author, and year of publication.

## 5. ACCESSING E-RESOURCES (MyLOFT)

### 5.1. Setup and Login
KCAU uses the MyLOFT (My Library on Finger Tips) platform for remote access to eBooks and journals.
* **Computers:** Use Chrome or Microsoft Edge and download the MyLOFT app/extension from https://app.myloft.xyz/
* **Mobile:** Download MyLOFT from Google Play Store or Apple App Store. (Requires Android 10+ or iOS 13+).
* **VPN:** For mobile, users must accept the VPN configuration and disable private DNS in phone settings.
* **Credentials:** Login using the official KCAU University email. New accounts are usually approved within 24 hours.

### 5.2. Downloading E-Books
Some E-books (specifically from ProQuest) require "Adobe Digital Editions (ADE)" software to view offline. This software monitors usage and manages copyright restrictions.

## 6. SUBSCRIBED DATABASES AND REPOSITORIES

### 6.1. Academic and Research Databases
* **Academic Journals:** Peer-reviewed open access journals covering humanities, engineering, medical, and social sciences.
* **DOAB (Directory of Open Access Books):** Searchable index to peer-reviewed monographs.
* **DOAJ (Directory of Open Access Journals):** Diverse index of global open access journals.
* **Duke University Press:** African studies, cultural studies, and gender studies.
* **EBSCOhost:** Over 16,500 journals across science, technology, and medicine.
* **Emerald Publishing:** Marketing, business, and engineering.
* **IEEE Xplore:** Electrical engineering, computer science, and robotics.
* **IMF eLibrary:** Economics, international finance, and statistics.
* **JSTOR:** Humanities, social sciences, and mathematics.
* **Mary Ann Liebert Inc.:** Biotechnology, regenerative medicine, and public health.
* **SAGE:** Humanities, social sciences, and research methods.
* **Taylor & Francis:** Behavioral science, law, and environment (5,000+ titles).
* **Wiley Online Library:** Business, law, and physics.

### 6.2. Open Access Repositories
* **University Institutional Repository:** https://repository.kcau.ac.ke (Theses and Dissertations).
* **Africa Information Highway (AIH):** Official and other statistics across Africa.
* **World Bank Open Knowledge Repository:** International economics and governance.
* **Kenya Law:** http://www.kenyalaw.org (Review and reform of Kenyan laws).

## 7. CIRCULATION SERVICES (BORROWING RULES)

### 7.1. Borrowing Eligibility
Users must have a Borrower's account. Professional program students must provide proof of caution fee payment. Borrowing privileges are non-transferable.

### 7.2. Borrowing Criteria (Loan Periods)
| Borrower Category | Max Items | Main Long Loan | Short Loan | Special Short Loan | Renewals |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Admin Staff | 5 | 30 Days | 14 Days | 3 Days | 1-2 Times |
| Teaching Staff | 5 | 30 Days | 14 Days | 3 Days | 1-2 Times |
| Postgraduate | 5 | 14 Days | 7 Days | 3 Days | 1-2 Times |
| Undergraduate | 3 | 14 Days | 7 Days | 3 Days | 1-2 Times |
| Professional | 1 | 14 Days | 7 Days | 3 Days | 1-2 Times |
| Part-time Profs | 6 | 30 Days | 14 Days | 3 Days | 1-2 Times |
| ICPAK Staff | 1 | 30 Days | 14 Days | 3 Days | 1-2 Times |

### 7.3. Fines and Penalties
* **Overdue Fines:** Ksh. 5 per item per day.
* **Blocked Accounts:** Accounts are automatically blocked if fines exceed KES 100/=.
* **Suspension:** Occurs for outstanding fines, failure to register for the current semester, or failure to return items from the previous semester.

### 7.4. Renewal Process
Renewals can be done on or before the due date via:
1. **Online (OPAC):** Login to http://opac.kcau.ac.ke with Student ID (username) and National ID (password).
2. **Email:** Write to the library with the Item Title and Call Number.
3. **In-Person:** Visit the circulation desk with the ID card.
*Note: Items cannot be renewed if they are overdue or placed on hold by another user.*

### 7.5. Lost or Damaged Items
Users must pay the replacement cost for lost or damaged items. For lost items, the borrower must purchase a similar item or seek guidance from the Head of Library Services.

## 8. CODE OF CONDUCT
* **ID Cards:** Must be presented at the security desk and the loan desk. Impersonation results in disciplinary action.
* **Prohibited Acts:** Smoking, eating, drinking, littering, and loitering.
* **Noise:** Cell phones must not be used in the library. Consultation and discussions are restricted to DESIGNATED AREAS.
* **Theft Prevention:** Any attempt to remove un-issued material triggers the Book Detection System and disciplinary action.
* **Personal Property:** The Library is NOT liable for the loss of personal property through negligence.
