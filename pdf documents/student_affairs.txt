### 1.7. Historical Background

KCA University dates back to **1989**, with the establishment of the institution as **Kenya College of Accountancy** by the Institute of Certified Public Accountants of Kenya (ICPAK), charged with the mandate to improve the quality of accountancy and financial management training in the country.

#### Key Milestones

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **1987/88** | British Government-funded study by Chart Foulks Lynch CIPFA Services (UK) revealed Kenya needed 400 qualified accountants annually; existing output was less than 100 |
| **1989** | College commenced services with 170 students in rented premises in Westlands |
| **Today** | Enrollment stands at over **5,000 students annually** |
| **Current** | Main Campus: Thika Road at Ruaraka; Satellite Campuses: Kitengela, Town Campus, Amagoro, and Kisumu |

### 3.3. Registration

#### 3.3.1 Registration of New Students

| Step | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| 1️⃣ | Present Admission letter as proof of admission |
| 2️⃣ | Complete KCAU registration form indicating subjects/units for trimester *(guided by respective faculties)* |
| 3️⃣ | Submit unit registration forms at Registrar's Office with proof of payment or Credit Control approval |
| 4️⃣ | Receive Student ID Card (Smart Card) upon filling card request form + proof of fee payment |
| 5️⃣ | Names must match application documents, affidavit, or National ID *(same order in all official records)* |
| 6️⃣ | Name changes during studies require legal procedures + official documents submitted to Registrar before graduation |

- 📅 Pre-registration opens **4 weeks** before new trimester/semester
- 🔄 Add/Drop (Swap) period: First **2 weeks** of new trimester/semester
- ⚠️ Students unable to register within period must apply for academic leave by **Week 6**

#### 3.3.3 Deferred Admission

| Scenario | Policy |
|----------|--------|
| 🎫 Slot Reservation | Maximum **3 trimesters (1 academic year)**; lapses thereafter |
| 📝 Deferment Request | Fill Student Request Form (Registrar's Office or Student Portal) |
| 📚 Academic Leave | Maximum **2 academic years**; apply one year at a time; requires Senate approval |
| 🔄 Leave Renewal | Must renew registration at beginning of every semester |
| 📉 Deregistration | Applies if academic leave not applied for within deadlines |

#### 3.3.4 Resumption of Studies / Readmission

1. Seek written approval via Office of the Registrar
2. Pay readmission fee before unit registration
3. Present formal approval from Registrar upon readmission
4. Students returning from disciplinary terms must see University Counselor, then Registrar

### 3.16. Requirements for Pass of a Unit/Subject

### 3.20. Conduct of Examinations

#### Permitted Items

| Item | Conditions |
|------|-----------|
| 📐 Mathematical tables/data books | Only under examiner-prescribed conditions |
| 🧮 Electronic calculator | Examiner-prescribed; must be portable, self-powered, noiseless, non-programmable |
| 🔋 Spare batteries | Allowed for calculators |

#### Prohibited Items

- ❌ Smart calculators or gadgets
- ❌ Audible alarms *(unless examiner-instructed)*
- ❌ Supplementary materials/operation manuals/annotations on calculators
- ❌ Written/printed materials *(unless Open Book/Restricted Book exam)*

#### Open Book vs. Restricted Book Exams

| Type | Rules |
|------|-------|
| **📖 Open Book** | Any written/printed materials allowed; no checking of items; materials cannot be attached to script |
| **🔒 Restricted Book** | Only examiner-specified materials; no annotations/writing; Senate approval required |
| **✍️ Restricted Book – May Be Written Upon** | Examiner-specified materials may be annotated; pre-prepared materials cannot be attached to script |

#### General Examination Rules

1. 🎒 All unapproved books, papers, instruments, personal belongings must be left where invigilator directs
2. 📄 All paper used during exam must be handed to invigilator before leaving
3. 🚪 Entry/exit not permitted **30 minutes after start** or **30 minutes before end** *(unless script surrendered)*
4. 🤫 No communication with other candidates during exam
5. ✍️ No writing after invigilator announces time expiration; no extra time for reading/amending scripts
6. 🔍 Invigilators may confiscate unauthorized materials and expel disruptive candidates

#### Examination Offences & Discipline

> Any complaint of examination offence referred to Disciplinary Committee of Senate.

**Process:**
1. Candidate notified in writing by Registrar (AA) of irregularity details, hearing time/place
2. Candidate may submit written response and attend hearing
3. Senate (or delegated Disciplinary Committee) may impose penalties:

| Penalty | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| 🚫 Disqualification | From sitting examinations for prescribed period |
| ❌ Cancellation | Of marks/grades for subject/examination where offence occurred |
| 🎓 Withdrawal | Of academic award(s) where offence occurred |
| ⚖️ Other Penalties | As prescribed in Student General Conduct Rules |

> 📬 All examination enquiries/reports directed to Registrar (Academic).
