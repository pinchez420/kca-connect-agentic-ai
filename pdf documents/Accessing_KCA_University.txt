## Arrival & Gate Access

### Getting to Campus
KCA University Main Campus
Thika Superhighway, Survey, Nairobi
Landmark: Next to CPA Centre


### Entry Protocol

| Student Category | Access Method | Requirements |
|-----------------|--------------|--------------|
| Continuing Students | Biometric scan **OR** Student ID card | Valid, activated ID |
| New Students | Temporary ID + Admission Letter | Issued during orientation |

> **No student is permitted entry without valid identification.**  
> *Student ID cards must be carried at all times while on campus.*

## Welcome Centre

> **Your first stop for all administrative services**  
> Located next to the Tuition Block

### Services Offered

| Service | Location | Details |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Finance Office | Ground Floor | Fee payments, statements, financial clearance, exam card processing |
| Cashier | Ground Floor | Cash transactions, official receipt issuance |
| Inquiry Desk | Entrance | General queries, directions, student support |
| Exam Cards | Finance Office | Printing, troubleshooting, collection |
| Student ID Services | Welcome Centre | Replacement *(Kshs. 600)*, collection of withheld cards |
| Washrooms | Throughout Block | Clean, accessible facilities |

### Student ID Card Management

| Scenario | Action Required | Cost/Notes |
|----------|----------------|------------|
| New Card Issuance | Reporting day for new students | Free |
| Lost/Damaged Replacement | Report immediately + fill form | **Kshs. 600** |
| Withheld Card Collection | Resolve underlying issue first | Free upon clearance |
| Activation | Upon fee payment confirmation | Automatic |

> *Your Student ID is required for: Library access, Exam entry, Campus facilities, Biometric attendance.*

## Important Rules & Reminders

### Student ID Card Rules *(From KCAU Rules & Regulations)*

| Rule | Consequence |
|------|-------------|
| Must carry ID at all times | Entry denied without ID |
| Exchanging ID cards | **PENALTY + SUSPENSION** |
| Lost card not reported immediately | Replacement fee: **Kshs. 600** |

### Fee Payment Rules

| Violation | Consequence |
|-----------|-------------|
| Found in class without paid fees | **EXPULSION** *(unless Finance Director authorization)* |
| Attending unpaid classes | **SUSPENSION** + full fee payment required |

###  Prohibited Conduct

| Prohibition | Consequence |
|-------------|-------------|
| Smoking in compound | Disciplinary committee + **SUSPENSION** |
| Smoking drugs (e.g., bhang) | **EXPULSION** |
| Drunkenness on campus | **Immediate SUSPENSION** |
| Fights, forgery, theft | Referred to police + **EXPULSION/SUSPENSION** |

### 📱 Mobile Phone Policy
📱 Mobile phones must NOT be put on while in class at all times
🎧 Walkmans may only be used in fields out of class hours
